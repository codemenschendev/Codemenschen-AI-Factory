What the labelled paid-social ads in the reference library do. A small sample, 32 ads with a
readable angle, so these are tendencies, not laws:

- The hook sits at the TOP of the creative in 23 of 32. Headline at the top, the call to action
  at the bottom, the picture carrying the middle.
- 18 of 32 show a person; 14 show the product or the place. A service is sold with the person it
  is for, a product with the product in use, never on white.
- Text load: 12 light, 15 medium, 5 heavy. Two lines and a button is the norm. A paragraph laid
  over a photograph is the exception and reads as a leaflet.
- Only 5 of 32 print a price. The offer angle sells a reason before it sells a number.
- 22 of 32 are square feed posts, 6 portrait, 3 stories. The square is the workhorse; in a story
  the picture does almost all of the work and the words are one line.
