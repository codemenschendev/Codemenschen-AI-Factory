# 08 — Objection Handling (Top 12 → FAQ source copy)

Principles: concede what's true immediately; explain the *why* behind every hard term; never counter an objection with a promise of outcomes. These answers are draft FAQ copy — final wording per language [COUNSEL REVIEW] where marked.

---

**1. "So I don't actually own the app?"**
Correct — and we want you to be completely clear on this before you pay. You buy an **exclusive license with revenue rights**: the app runs for you alone, and its revenue is yours under the license terms. You do not own the intellectual property, and you are not buying a company or shares. Why this structure? Because we keep the IP, we can keep building, fixing, and operating the app for you — that's what your retainer pays for. If you want to exit, you can resell the license on our platform (5% handover fee). [COUNSEL REVIEW: exact license description.]

**2. "I'm locked into you for development. Isn't that a red flag?"**
It's real lock-in and we won't pretend otherwise. The trade: one accountable studio that built the app, validated it, and commits to an agreed, published roadmap — instead of you briefing freelancers on code you can't read. You always have an exit: relist and resell your license. Before buying, read the roadmap for your app; that document is what the lock-in actually binds *us* to.

**3. "What if the app fails?"**
It can, and you can lose what you put in. Validation with real paying users lowers the risk of building something nobody wants — it does not remove market risk. If an app stops being worth running, you can relist it for resale at a price you choose; it may sell for more, less, or not at all. We publish exactly how failure, suspension, and resale work before you buy — no surprises is the one thing we can guarantee.

**4. "Why can't I talk to a human before buying?"**
Deliberate design. Self-serve is why licenses cost €500–5,000 instead of agency rates, and it means every buyer gets identical terms — nothing is negotiated in private, so everything is published in public: the contract, the pricing math, the validation evidence, this FAQ. If something isn't answered on the site, that's our bug — write to us and we'll answer in writing and usually add it here.

**5. "Is this an investment? Is it even legal?"**
It is a license purchase, not a security, share, or fund — you're buying usage and revenue rights to a specific app plus ongoing services. We're an Austrian company (Impressum below) operating under Austrian and EU consumer law, including your 14-day withdrawal right, which we honor. **6. "If the app already makes money, why would you sell the license?"**
Because our business is building and operating many apps on retainers — not running a portfolio of our own products. We're a development studio: we're good at shipping and running software, and the license + retainer model lets us do that at scale. You can check who we are: we've operated as Codemenschen in Vienna for years.

**7. "What exactly does the monthly retainer cover?"**
Development and operation under the roadmap agreed for your app: hosting, maintenance, updates, and the listed roadmap items. It's 5–10% of your purchase price per month — always shown in euros, with the yearly total, before checkout. Each app's roadmap is published on its listing page. If the retainer isn't paid, the license is suspended and the app is relisted; if it resells, you receive the resale surplus minus outstanding retainer. [COUNSEL REVIEW: suspension terms.]

**8. "Can I sell my license later? What will it be worth?"**
Yes — relisting on the platform is built in; on a successful handover we take 5%. What it's worth then, we honestly don't know: it may be more, less, or it may not find a buyer. We will never quote you expected resale values.

**9. "How do I know the 'real paying users' numbers are true?"**
Don't take our word — audit our process. Our validation methodology is published: test length, how users were recruited, what they paid, and what evidence each listing must show (anonymized payment/analytics records, date-stamped). We also publish the validations that **failed** — apps we built that didn't pass and were never listed.

**11. "5–10% a month adds up. Over two years that's more than the license."**
Correct, and here's the math on every listing before you buy: e.g. a €2,400 license at 5% is €120/month — €1,440/year. That's the full cost of having a professional team develop, host, and operate your app; there are no other fees except the optional ad budget you control and 5% if you resell. If ongoing professional operation isn't worth that to you, this product isn't a fit — genuinely.

**12. "If real users already pay for it, why is it only €500–5,000?"**
Because these are small, young apps at the very beginning — a few weeks of real paying users is meaningful evidence, not a mature business. The price reflects build scope and validation strength, not a valuation of future revenue. The low price is the honest signal of exactly how early you are.
