# 06 — Trust Strategy

Context: zero traction, novel category, self-serve, four-figure purchase. Trust is the product before the product. Strategy: **verifiability over persuasion** — every trust claim must be checkable by the visitor without trusting us.

## Trust asset inventory

| Asset | Why it works | How to deploy |
|---|---|---|
| Real paying users per app (historical actuals) | Only evidence of demand a skeptic can't dismiss as opinion | Per-listing: "N users paid €X during a 6-week test", with methodology link and anonymized raw evidence (receipts/analytics screenshots, PII removed) |
| One buyer per app | Structural exclusivity that is also literally true — scarcity without manipulation | Stated as plain fact; licensed apps shown as "Licensed", never dramatized |
| Plain-language failure & suspension terms | Scammers hide downside; we headline it. Strongest single differentiator | Homepage risk block, Pricing page, FAQ, checkout summary [COUNSEL REVIEW] |
| Public standard contract | Removes "what's in the fine print" fear entirely | Full contract PDF/HTML linked pre-purchase, DE+EN [COUNSEL REVIEW] |
| Codemenschen.at — real Vienna company | Established firm with clients, address, history; not an anonymous landing page | About page with team names/photos, link to codemenschen.at, years operating, existing client work |
| Impressum + Austrian/EU legal frame | ECG-mandated identity + GDPR + consumer protection = jurisdictional trust | Impressum, Datenschutz, AGB complete at launch; EU 14-day withdrawal honored and advertised [COUNSEL REVIEW] |
| Published validation methodology | Turns "trust our numbers" into "audit our process" (see 07) | Standalone methodology page, versioned, linked from every listing |
| Build-in-public log | Ongoing, dated, unfakeable-at-scale evidence of a real operation | Werkstatt log: validation runs incl. failures, listings, roadmap updates (see 07) |
| Post-purchase dashboard with historical ad/usage actuals | Proves ongoing reporting honesty; referenced (not shown) pre-purchase | Screenshot of dashboard *structure* (not numbers) on marketing pages |
| Self-serve = published everything | "No sales calls" reframed: nothing is negotiable or hidden, everyone gets the same terms | Explicit copy: "There's no salesperson because there's nothing a salesperson would know that isn't on this site" |

## Trust-risk register

| # | Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pattern-match to "passive income" scams (novel offer + money + internet) | Critical | Banned-vocabulary list enforced (02); risk disclosure adjacent to every value claim; no earnings talk anywhere; craft tone; visible Austrian identity |
| 2 | "You own nothing" backlash — buyer feels license ≠ what they imagined | Critical | "License, not IP, not equity" repeated verbatim on homepage, listing, checkout; checkout comprehension checkpoint (plain-language summary buyer must scroll/acknowledge); 14-day withdrawal as safety valve |
| 3 | Suspension/forfeiture mechanic perceived (or ruled) unfair | High | [COUNSEL REVIEW — unfair terms, B2C]; plain-language pre-purchase disclosure; resale-surplus-to-buyer term emphasized as the fairness component; grace period + reminder process before suspension (recommend defining one) |
| 4 | Regulatory reclassification (KMG/prospectus — "revenue rights" sold to consumers) | Critical | [COUNSEL REVIEW before launch — blocking]; never use investment vocabulary; document counsel's guidance as copy constraints |
| 5 | Validation numbers doubted ("you paid those users yourself") | High | Methodology publishes test design incl. how testers were recruited; anonymized evidence; publish failed validations too — failures make the successes credible |
| 6 | Zero social proof at launch (empty catalog effect) | High | Process transparency instead of testimonials (07); "we're new — here's everything about how we work" honesty post; small catalog framed as curation, which is also true |
| 7 | Platform-continuity fear ("what if Codemenschen folds?") | Medium-High | Written continuity answer in FAQ [COUNSEL REVIEW: license survival/escrow options]; Codemenschen's operating history as evidence |
| 8 | Lock-in resentment post-purchase → public complaints | Medium | Lock-in disclosed with its rationale pre-purchase (04); roadmap accountability in dashboard; resale exit always available and functional |
| 9 | First resale/failure handled badly in public | Medium | Pre-write the operational playbook for suspension, relisting, surplus payout BEFORE first sale; first real case study published honestly, whatever the outcome |
| 10 | DE/EN legal or copy divergence | Medium | Single-source content model; DE governs legally [COUNSEL REVIEW]; every compliance-sensitive string reviewed in both languages |
| 11 | Overclaiming by future marketing/ads (agency drift) | Medium | This doc set is the compliance contract for all channels; ad copy uses only approved phrases (02); no ad may cite performance numbers |

## Trust sequencing

- **Launch:** identity assets (Impressum, About, codemenschen.at link), public contract, methodology v1, risk-forward copy.
- **Months 1–6:** build-in-public cadence; publish first failed validation; first buyer story only with consent and only process-factual (no earnings).
- **First resale or failure event:** publish the full honest case study — this converts the scariest terms (suspension, resale) into demonstrated fairness, the highest trust asset the platform can ever own.
