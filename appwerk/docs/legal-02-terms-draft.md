> **Status:** DRAFT — plain-language skeleton written by the founding team. Every clause requires counsel red-line before any part appears on the live site. Written deliberately in the same plain language as the website: the contract and the marketing must never tell two different stories.

## 1. Parties and subject
Codemenschen.at (Vienna, Austria — full Impressum data here) grants the Licensee an **exclusive license** to operate and receive the revenue of one specified application. The Company retains all code and intellectual property and is the exclusive provider of development, operations, and promotion services.

## 2. What the Licensee receives
- Sole licensee status for the specified app; no other license to that app will be granted while this one is in force.
- All net revenue generated by the app (definition of "net revenue" — payment-processor fees, store commissions, taxes — enumerated here, no surprises).
- A dashboard with historical performance data: users, revenue, costs, roadmap status.
- Roadmap approval rights: no roadmap phase begins without the Licensee's approval. [Counsel: see KMG question 3 — the strength of this right matters regulatorily.]

## 3. What the Licensee pays
- One-time license price as stated on the listing.
- Monthly retainer of the percentage stated on the listing (5–10% of license price), funding the approved roadmap. Itemized monthly statement provided.
- Optional advertising budget, only when and as instructed by the Licensee.
- 5% handover fee on a completed resale (§ 7).

## 5. Retainer default, suspension, relisting [COUNSEL REVIEW — unfair-terms sensitive]
Plain-language mechanic, with consumer-protective steps added:
1. Missed retainer payment → written notice, **30-day cure period**. During cure: license intact, roadmap paused.
2. Pause option [APPROVED by operator]: Licensee may switch to a **minimal-maintenance retainer** (keeps the app alive, no new development) instead of defaulting. Standard retainer remains 5–10%; maintenance tier proposed at ~2–3% of license price [operator to confirm exact rate].
3. After uncured default: license suspended; app relisted for resale.
4. On resale: former Licensee receives **resale proceeds minus (a) outstanding retainer amounts and (b) the 5% handover fee**. Statement of account provided. No other deductions.
5. If not resold within 12 months [operator decision]: the app is **delisted** and the suspended license terminates; the former Licensee's claim to resale proceeds ends with delisting. ## 7. Resale
Licensee may list the license on the platform at any time. Full, current performance history is attached to the resale listing (same transparency the Licensee received). Company approval of the buyer limited to enumerated grounds (sanctions/AML, payment capability) — not discretionary. 5% handover fee on completion.

## 8. What the Company does not promise
No revenue, profit, resale price, or app-store approval outcome is guaranteed. Validation data is historical fact, not a forecast. This clause is also on the website in the same words.
