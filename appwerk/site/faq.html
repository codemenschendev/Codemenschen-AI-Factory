What you're actually getting

Do I actually own the app?

You own an exclusive license and its revenue — not the code, IP, or a company. The difference matters, so we say it everywhere.

What does "exclusive license" mean concretely?

The app is licensed to you and nobody else. Its revenue flows to you. It appears under its own identity in the stores. Appwerk retains the code and IP and remains the sole development team — that's the trade the model is built on: you get a professional team without hiring one; we keep the technical estate we're responsible for.

Is this an investment product?

Legally, the classification of revenue-bearing licenses under Austrian law is being reviewed by counsel before launch [COUNSEL REVIEW]. Practically: treat it with the same seriousness as any decision where money is at risk. Nothing on this platform is investment advice, and no returns are promised.

Money and risk

Can I lose money?

Yes. AI analysis lowers the risk of building something nobody wants — it doesn't guarantee success.

What is the monthly retainer actually spent on?

Development hours, design, servers and infrastructure, app-store operations, and monthly reporting — under the roadmap you approved. It's 5–10% of your purchase price depending on the app's complexity, stated on every listing before you buy. It is not a platform fee; it's the working budget of your app.

Will you tell me how much I could earn?

No — and be wary of anyone who will. We publish the AI's analysis (clearly marked as potential) and, once live, the real revenue and cost history in your dashboard. Potential is not a promise.

How does the ad budget work?

Promotion is optional and separately funded — you decide if and when to add budget. We recommend campaigns based on what comparable past campaigns actually did, reported as historical numbers with their context, never as a forecast of yours.

Leaving, selling, failing

Can I take the app to another developer?

No — development stays with our team; that's the model. Your exit is reselling the license on the platform (5% fee).

How does reselling my license work?

You list your license on the platform with the app's full, real performance history attached — the next buyer sees exactly what you saw, updated. Appwerk charges a 5% handover fee on the sale. The price is between you and the buyer; we never guarantee one.

What happens if I stop paying the retainer?

Your license is suspended and the app is relisted for a new licensee. If it resells, you receive the proceeds minus outstanding retainer costs. We'd rather you resell deliberately than lapse — talk to us before you stop paying; pausing a roadmap is often possible.

What if the app just doesn't work out?

Some won't — we say that everywhere. Your options: fund a roadmap change, reduce the roadmap to minimal maintenance, resell the license at whatever the market gives, or let it lapse. What we will never do is dress a failing app up as a healthy one; the dashboard shows what it shows.

What happens to my license if Appwerk itself shuts down?

A company-continuity provision (e.g. code escrow or license conversion) is being defined with counsel and will be part of the standard contract before launch [COUNSEL REVIEW]. It's a fair question and it deserves a contractual answer, not a reassuring paragraph.

Buying process

Do I have a right of withdrawal after buying?

Your app is finished as a one-off work, customised specifically to you (name, branding, roadmap), and that custom work starts right after payment. At checkout you expressly request this immediate start and acknowledge that your 14-day withdrawal right expires with the start of performance, as EU law provides (§ 18 FAGG). This is stated plainly before you pay; classification and wording are under legal review [COUNSEL REVIEW].

Why is there no sales call?

Everything a decision needs is published on the listing. If something's missing, that's a bug — tell us.

Where do the numbers come from?

From our automated AI analysis of app-store data: demand, reviews, price points, category benchmarks. All revenue figures are potential — not measurements.

What about taxes?

License revenue is generally taxable income, and treatment varies by country and personal situation. We provide clean revenue and cost statements from your dashboard; please consult a tax advisor — we don't give tax advice.
