License & Marketplace Terms

⚠ DRAFT — this page is a plain-language skeleton written by the founding team. Every clause requires review by Austrian counsel before any part of it is in force. Nothing here is a binding contract yet. It is published anyway, because the contract and the marketing must never tell two different stories.

1. Parties and subject

Codemenschen.at (Vienna, Austria — full company details in the Impressum) grants the Licensee an exclusive license to operate and receive the revenue of one specified application. The Company retains all code and intellectual property and is the exclusive provider of development, operations, and promotion services.

2. What the Licensee receives

  • Sole licensee status for the specified app — no other license to that app will be granted while this one is in force.
  • All net revenue generated by the app. "Net revenue" is defined exhaustively in the contract (payment-processor fees, store commissions, taxes) — no surprises.
  • A dashboard with historical performance data: users, revenue, costs, roadmap status.
  • Roadmap approval rights: no roadmap phase begins without the Licensee's approval. [COUNSEL REVIEW]

3. What the Licensee pays

  • A one-time license price, as stated on the listing.
  • A monthly retainer of the percentage stated on the listing (5–10% of the license price), funding the approved roadmap. An itemized monthly statement is provided.
  • An optional advertising budget — only when and as instructed by the Licensee. €0 is a valid choice.
  • A 5% handover fee on a completed resale (§ 7).

4. Your 14-day withdrawal right [COUNSEL REVIEW — core item]

As a consumer in the EU you have a 14-day right of withdrawal. At checkout you choose one of two paths — both are honest, and the choice is yours:

  1. Immediate start: you expressly request that Appwerk begins performance (license activation and roadmap start) right away, and acknowledge that you thereby lose the 14-day withdrawal right, as EU/Austrian law (FAGG) provides. This requires a separate, unticked checkbox — we never pre-tick it — and you receive written confirmation of your waiver by email.
  2. Deferred start: you keep your full 14-day withdrawal right, and license activation and roadmap start are deferred until the period has expired.

The full statutory withdrawal instruction is on the withdrawal page.

5. If the retainer isn't paid: cure, pause, suspension, relisting [COUNSEL REVIEW]

  1. A missed retainer payment triggers a written notice and a 30-day cure period. During that period your license stays intact; the roadmap is paused.
  2. Pause option: instead of defaulting, you may switch to a minimal-maintenance retainer (keeps the app alive, no new development), proposed at roughly 2–3% of the license price. [final rate pending]
  3. After an uncured default, the license is suspended and the app is relisted for resale.
  4. If it resells, you receive the resale proceeds minus (a) outstanding retainer amounts and (b) the 5% handover fee — with a statement of account. No other deductions.
  5. If the app is not resold within 12 months, it is delisted and the suspended license ends; the claim to resale proceeds ends with delisting. [COUNSEL REVIEW]

6. What happens if Appwerk itself fails

A continuity provision (contract annex) governs what happens to your license if the Company ceases operations — including source-code escrow and a conversion of your license so it survives the Company. [COUNSEL REVIEW — insolvency-law construction pending]

7. Resale

You may list your license on the platform at any time. The full, current performance history is attached to the resale listing — the next buyer sees exactly what you saw. The Company may reject a buyer only on enumerated grounds (sanctions/AML, payment capability), not at its discretion. A 5% handover fee applies on completion.

8. What the Company does not promise

No revenue, profit, resale price, or app-store approval outcome is guaranteed. Validation data is historical fact, not a forecast. This clause is also on the website — in the same words.

9. Term, termination, governing law

Notice periods for the retainer, consumer-protection constraints on duration and termination, Austrian law with mandatory consumer-home-jurisdiction rules, and the EU ODR-platform reference will be set out here after counsel review. [COUNSEL REVIEW]
