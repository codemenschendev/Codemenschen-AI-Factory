About

Software people, accountable by name.

Appwerk is built and operated by codemenschen.at, a software company in Vienna. It started with one question: why can only technical people or wealthy acquirers hold a stake in an app?

Why Appwerk exists

Plenty of people understand products and want a stake in the app economy — but can't code, hire developers, or run infrastructure. Every existing route in requires exactly that.

So we built a third route: our AI analyzes the app store and prepares the most promising concepts, we build them, and license each one exclusively. You fund the roadmap; our team builds, operates, and promotes. Not a fund, not a company purchase — one specific app, with a professional team attached.

How the AI analysis works — the short version

Our AI analyzes app-store data across a category: demand, review complaints, price points, competitor gaps. The most promising concepts get prepared as listings with clear KPIs — build cost, marketing cost, possible monthly revenue. All revenue figures are potential, not measurements.

The honest limits

We're good at building and operating software — not at telling the future. Some apps will fail; licensees can lose what they put in. That's why the risks are on our homepage, not in a PDF nobody opens.
