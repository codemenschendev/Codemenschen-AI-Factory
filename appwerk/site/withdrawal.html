Your right of withdrawal

⚠ DRAFT — the statutory withdrawal instruction (Widerrufsbelehrung) and its exact legal basis are being finalized with Austrian counsel. What follows is the plain-language version of how it will work. It is not yet the legally operative text.

Model update: every app is finished as a one-off work customised to the buyer (name, branding, roadmap), and the custom work begins immediately after payment. At checkout you expressly request this immediate start and acknowledge that the 14-day withdrawal right expires with the start of performance (§ 18 FAGG). Note for counsel: the "custom-made" exception (§ 18 Abs 1 Z 3 FAGG) covers goods, not digital content — the expiry-on-performance route (Z 1/Z 11) is the intended basis and must be confirmed. The right itself

If you buy as a consumer in the EU, you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The period starts on the day the contract is concluded. To withdraw, you send us an unambiguous statement (email is enough) before the period expires; we confirm receipt and refund all payments received without undue delay. The choice you make at checkout

A license only makes sense once we activate it and start the roadmap — which is "beginning performance" in legal terms. EU law (in Austria: FAGG) lets you choose between two honest paths, and the checkout presents both:

  1. Start now, waive withdrawal. You tick a separate, never pre-ticked checkbox that reads: "I expressly request that Appwerk begins performance (license activation and roadmap start) immediately, and I acknowledge that I thereby lose my 14-day right of withdrawal." You then receive confirmation of this waiver by email, on a durable medium, as the law requires.
  2. Keep the withdrawal right, start later. If you don't tick the box, license activation and roadmap start are deferred by 14 days and your withdrawal right stays fully intact. Nothing about your license is worse — it just starts later.

We will never nudge you toward the waiver. Both buttons carry equal visual weight at checkout — the choice is genuinely yours.
