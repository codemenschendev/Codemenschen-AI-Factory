Who is responsible

Codemenschen.at, Vienna, Austria (full contact details in the Impressum) is the controller for data processed on this website.

What this site processes today

  • No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts.
  • Language preference. Your DE/EN choice is stored in your browser's localStorage, on your device only. It is never transmitted to us.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard access logs (IP address, timestamp, requested page) for security and operations. [provider and retention period to be named — COUNSEL REVIEW]
  • Web fonts. All fonts are hosted on our own server. No request to Google Fonts or any other third-party font service occurs when you visit this site.
  • Email. If you email us (e.g. a question about a listing), we process your address and message to answer you — nothing else.

What will be added before launch

  • A waitlist / new-listing notification signup (email address, double opt-in, unsubscribe in every message — transactional-style only, no drip campaigns).
  • Checkout and payment processing (a named payment provider will process payment data; we will not store card numbers).
  • The buyer dashboard (account data for licensees).

Each of these gets its own section in the counsel-reviewed notice — with legal basis, retention period, and processor — before it goes live. [COUNSEL REVIEW]

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to complain to the Austrian data protection authority (dsb.gv.at). Write to us via the contact in the Impressum and we will act on it.
